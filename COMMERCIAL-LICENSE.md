Examples: embedding the software or a derivative in a product or service,

beyond the evaluation described below, redistribution as part of a commercial
offering, and paid consulting or integration work built on it.

## Evaluation license

A free, time-limited evaluation license for assessing the software for a
possible commercial use is available on request.

## How to ask

Write to unclearness.dev@gmail.com with:

- your organization and contact person,
- the intended use (product, service, internal tool, research prototype),
- the expected scale (deployments, number of sensors or sites, users),
- whether the software would be redistributed or embedded, and
- the timeline.

Scope, term, fees, redistribution rights, patent grant, warranty disclaimers
and support are agreed per contract.
